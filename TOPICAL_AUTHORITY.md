# Topical Authority — live.panggung.id

## Role and boundary

live.panggung.id should become a practical Indonesian-language production guide for teams planning, procuring, delivering, monitoring, and closing live-streamed and hybrid events. Editorial pages support the site's commercial live-streaming, event-type, rental, package, and portfolio routes; they do not reproduce city pages, sell equipment, restate service packages, or replace the existing `/platform-live-streaming/` and `/peralatan-yang-digunakan-untuk-live-streaming-profesional.html` pages.

Live production combines electrical, structural, rigging, crowd, fire, weather, audiovisual, network, platform, copyright, privacy, and accessibility risks. Every load, capacity, clearance, power, acoustic, image, latency, bitrate, availability, safety, permit, insurance, rights, price, or performance claim is an evidence gate. Authors must use current Indonesian authority requirements and authoritative standards where applicable, venue rules, engineer-approved rigging documents, electrical test records, exact manufacturer/platform documentation, measured network and audiovisual tests, and project records. Qualified riggers, structural and electrical professionals, crowd/fire/safety specialists, network engineers, and legal/rights advisers remain responsible for specialist judgments.

## Context used

- `sitemap-complete.xml` contains 1,062 unique URLs, matching the tracked HTML-route count.
- The inventory includes 488 `live-streaming-<location>` pages and 488 `sewa-alat-streaming-<location>` pages: 976 geographic templates, or about 92% of the sitemap.
- Non-geographic inventory includes 13 live-streaming hub/event-type routes, six rental hub/equipment routes, 40 category/archive routes, seven author routes, four news pagination routes, four generator routes, two Elementor template routes, and ten core or other pages.
- Commercial event routes cover seminar, workshop, graduation, wedding, corporate, launching, entertainment, virtual, podcast, government, religious, and exhibition events. These retain service intent.
- Rental routes cover camera, lighting, sound system, video switcher, and event internet; `/paket-harga/` owns package/price intent.
- Homepage claims such as professional service, maximum results, stable internet, or hassle-free delivery were treated as promotional hypotheses, not evidence.

## Ignored template noise

Geographic keyword swaps, category and author archives, pagination, generator/template surfaces, repeated bodies, navigation/footer copy, and metadata were excluded from topic discovery. Event-type service pages are commercial destinations rather than proof of production capability. Equipment-rental pages and the existing equipment article own broad rental and equipment-overview intent; new editorial briefs address specific planning or verification jobs instead.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links to its parent hub and the two or three sibling guides required for the same production decision. Cross-topic links follow the lifecycle: LVE-01 brief → LVE-02/LVE-03 venue and risk → LVE-04–LVE-12 systems → LVE-13 rehearsal → LVE-14 live control → LVE-18 closeout, with LVE-15/LVE-16 as accessibility, rights, privacy, and security gates throughout. Procurement articles link to `/paket-harga/`, `/livestreaming/`, or the relevant `/sewa/` route only when a reader is ready to compare a commercial offer. Event-type routes remain service pages, and no article links to location templates merely to vary anchor text.
